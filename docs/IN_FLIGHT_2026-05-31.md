# In-flight work — 2026-05-31

What's running right now and what I'm trying to accomplish with it.

## In flight: M5 retrain of BIBO_2x

**Goal:** reduce the BIBO_2x super-res CNN's mid-frequency
over-smoothing on out-of-distribution textures, specifically Z8Z_6693
(mixed-contrast). The currently-shipped checkpoint
`bibo2x_ane_ml2_q3_dec2_diverse` produces a rendered LPIPS of 0.343 on
that gate image — way over even PREVIEW's 0.15 ceiling. Bayer fidelity
is fine (40.39 dB, clears UPRESABLE), but the rendered comparison
reveals the CNN smoothing grain that the colorist would otherwise
keep.

**Run:**
- Host: `gpr-m5` (192.168.1.162, Apple M5 Max, separate machine — not
  this M3 Max).
- PID: 97791 (verify with `ssh gpr-m5 'ps -p 97791 -o pid,etime'`).
- Log: `gpr-m5:/tmp/train_bibo2x_gateclean.log`.
- Variant: `F_ane` (2× super-res; same arch as the current champion).
- Corpus: `tiles_ml2_q3_dec2_combined_with_ood_gateclean.npz`
  (22 960 tiles, 574 sources; Z8Z_0067 dropped — that was the lone
  gate-image leak, which explains why its bayer_psnr_final was an
  inflated 57.45 dB while the other three gate images were 40–46 dB).
- Loss: `L1 + 0.5 × (1 − MS-SSIM)` (MSSSIM_LOSS_WEIGHT=0.5). Direct
  pressure to preserve structural detail vs. the L1-only smoothing.
- Held-out val: Z8Z_0002.
- Schedule: 80 epochs, batch 8, lr 5e-4, patience 25, cosine LR.
- Output: `gpr-m5:/Users/dcliftreaves/gpr_data/models/BayInBayOut_2x_AAon_w16_ANE_ML2_q3_dec2_msssim_gateclean.pt`.
- ETA: ~3.5 h from launch (started ~23:42 local). Per-epoch ~200 s.

**Success criterion:** when the run finishes, evaluate the new
checkpoint through the UPRESABLE gate. Production-grade means
`bayer_psnr_final ≥ 35 dB` (already a given for any reasonable
checkpoint) AND, ideally, rendered LPIPS on Z8Z_6693 drops below 0.15.
If it does, that's a candidate to replace the current
`bibo2x_ane_ml2_q3_dec2_diverse` champion in the registry.

If LPIPS doesn't move — or moves the wrong way — the next experiment
is grain-blend at render-time (option 2 from the original triage),
not another retrain.
